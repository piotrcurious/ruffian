What makes it rough?

Syncopation — land between the expected pulses.

Anticipation — arrive just before a kick or snare.

Reply — answer a hit without sitting on top of it.

Negative space — leave room for the sample to speak.

Collision control — exact overlaps are penalised unless Wreck mode is selected.
